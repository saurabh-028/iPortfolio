01 Live Project

ShastraShaw

A multilingual legal AI platform, running live at shastrashaw.online

Production RAG platform giving self-represented citizens grounded legal guidance across criminal, traffic, rental, and matrimonial law, in English, Hindi, and Marathi. A multi-layer guardrail stack (anti-hallucination prompting, cross-encoder reranking, citation verification with auto-retry) lifted automated faithfulness from 0.33 to 0.88 and Hit@5 from 93% to 100% on a 4-domain benchmark. An LLMOps pipeline (RAGAS plus lawyer-reviewed benchmarking) more than tripled context precision (0.21 to 0.73). Run as production infrastructure on AWS with CloudWatch alerting, rate-limiting, and automated backups. Validated by 20 legal professionals (3.94/5.0).
